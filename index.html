Mono

A personal task, habit and journal app for Windows, Android and iPhone.

Mono keeps a day in one place: tasks, planned time blocks, habits, a journal, sleep and a calendar view. It is a private, single-user app made by its owner for their own use.

Google Calendar

If you connect Google Calendar, Mono shows your calendar events alongside your tasks, and adds your planned and tracked tasks to a separate calendar it creates called “Mono”. You can disconnect at any time.
