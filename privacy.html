Privacy policy

Mono · last updated 27 September 2026

Mono is a personal productivity app used by its owner. This policy explains what it does with your data, including data from Google.

What Mono stores

Your tasks, habits, journal entries, sleep records and settings are stored on your own devices and, if you sign in, in your own sync account so your devices stay in step. They are not visible to anyone else.

Google Calendar data

  • Reading: Mono reads your calendar events so it can show them in its calendar view.
  • Writing: Mono creates, updates and deletes events only in a calendar it creates called “Mono”, to mirror your planned and tracked tasks. It does not change events in your other calendars.
  • Google data is used only to provide these features to you. It is not sold, not shared with third parties, not used for advertising, and not used to train AI or machine-learning models.

Mono's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Use Disconnect in Mono's Calendar settings, or remove Mono at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the “Mono” calendar in Google removes the events Mono created.
